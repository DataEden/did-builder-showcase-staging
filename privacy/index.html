    Privacy Policy

    Effective Date: March 2026

    DataInsideData™ LLC (“we”, “our”, or “us”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected and used when you visit this website.

    Information We Collect

    We may collect limited technical information automatically when you visit the site, including:

    • IP address (anonymized where applicable.)
    • Browser type and version
    • Device type
    • Pages visited
    • Time spent on pages
    • Referring websites

    This information is collected for analytics and performance improvement purposes.

    We do not collect personally identifiable information unless you voluntarily provide it (for example, by emailing us).

    Analytics

    We use Google Analytics 4 (GA4) to understand how visitors interact with this website.

    Google Analytics may use cookies, identifiers, and similar technologies to collect usage data such as pages visited, approximate location, device type, browser type, referral sources, and general interaction patterns. This information helps us understand traffic patterns, improve site performance, and make the website more useful to visitors.

    Google may process this information according to its own policies and systems. Visitors can learn more about how Google uses information from sites and apps that use Google services through Google’s published privacy resources.

    Visitors can also manage cookies through their browser settings or use Google’s available opt-out tools where applicable.

    For more information on how Google processes data, visit: https://policies.google.com/privacy

    You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking by using the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on: https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout

    Cookies

    This site may use cookies or similar technologies for:

    • analytics and traffic measurement,
    • performance monitoring,
    • basic site functionality,
    • security and abuse prevention,
    • advertising and ad measurement, if ads are enabled.

    You can control, block, or delete cookies through your browser settings. Please note that disabling cookies may affect how some websites function.

    Third-Party Services

    We may link to or embed third-party services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those external sites.

    Advertising

    This website may use Google AdSense or other advertising services to display ads.

    Third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads based on a visitor’s prior visits to this website or other websites. These technologies may help provide relevant ads, measure ad performance, and prevent fraud or abuse.

    Users can manage or opt out of personalized advertising through their Google ad settings or through browser-level cookie controls. Some third-party advertising vendors may also provide their own opt-out mechanisms.

    If advertising is enabled on this site, it will be used to support the operation, maintenance, and continued development of DataInsideData.com.

    Data Security

    We take the security of our website and its supporting infrastructure seriously and apply appropriate safeguards to protect against unauthorized access or misuse.

    As with any internet-based service, inherent risks exist; however, we actively work to maintain a secure and resilient platform.

    Technical and Security Metadata

    When users submit a Builder Showcase project or interact with site forms, DataInsideData™ may process limited technical metadata to support security, abuse prevention, troubleshooting, and platform reliability.

    This may include browser or user-agent information, request origin or referring page, submission timestamps, form validation results, and security-related request signals.

    This information is used to help prevent spam or duplicate submissions, protect the submission workflow, diagnose platform issues, and maintain the quality of the Builder Showcase.

    This information is not used to sell personal data or create advertising profiles.
